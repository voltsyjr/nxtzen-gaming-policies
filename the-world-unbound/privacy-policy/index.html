The World Unbound - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how the game The World Unbound (Android app com.worldunbound.game) handles your information. The game is made and published by NxtZen Gaming.

We do not collect any data

The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information or any other data about you or your device. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

What stays on your phone

Your saved game and your settings (such as sound and frame rate) are stored only on your phone, inside the app's own storage. They never leave your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, used for short buzzes when your hero is hit. You can turn vibration off in the game's settings.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a later version of the game changes how data is handled (for example by adding ads), this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: write to NxtZen Gaming at the developer email address shown on the game's Google Play page.
